HOW TO PLAY

1. Pick QUICK PASS or POWER RUN.
2. Press SNAP BALL.
3. Move with W A S D.
4. Click a glowing receiver to throw.
5. Keep running right after a catch.

On defense, move toward the ball and press F. Player 2 uses arrows, /, ., and Shift.

How to play

Pick any two of the 16 fictional SEC-inspired schools. In local mode, both people use one keyboard. The offensive player moves the QB, chooses a receiver, and throws. Once caught, the receiver is controlled with the same movement keys. The defender moves the highlighted defender, switches defenders, tackles, and can intercept a pass.

Passes travel through the air and can be caught or picked. Sprint briefly boosts speed, then stamina recovers. The CPU chooses targets, throws, tackles, and tries to score. A first down requires 10 yards; four missed downs cause a turnover. Touchdowns score 6 and grant an automatic extra point. A game ends after four 90-second quarters.

Dynasty saves in this browser. Recruiting and portal bids draw from your NIL budget. Offers resolve when you advance the season. Ratings from signed players improve your team. No internet connection is needed after the page loads.
